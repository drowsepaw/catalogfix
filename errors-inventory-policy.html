Inventory policy must be "deny" or "continue"

⚠️ Variant Inventory Policy contains "DENY" — the value must be exact.

Variant Inventory Policy controls what happens when stock runs out: deny stops selling, continue keeps selling. Shopify accepts only those two words, in lowercase, with nothing extra.

  • DENY, Deny → deny
  • Yes, true, allow → not valid; choose deny or continue
  • Trailing spaces ("deny ") also fail — trim the cell.

The same exactness applies to its neighbours: Variant Inventory Tracker must be shopify (lowercase) or blank, and Variant Fulfillment Service is normally manual.
