"Variant Price" is not a valid number

⚠️ Variant Price contains "$1,299.00" — Shopify prices must be plain numbers.

Shopify's CSV importer is strict about money: Variant Price, Variant Compare At Price and Cost per item must be plain numbers like 1299.00. Currency symbols, thousands separators and text all make the row fail.

The usual offenders

  • $1,299.00 → 1299.00 (drop the symbol and the thousands comma)
  • 12,99 (European decimal comma) → 12.99
  • USD 45 or 45 EUR → 45
  • Blank price on the main variant row — every variant needs a price

Fix it by hand

  1. Strip any currency symbol or code.
  2. Remove commas that separate thousands; convert a decimal comma to a decimal point.
  3. Keep at most two decimal places.

Watch the compare-at price too: if it ends up lower than the price, the product will not display as on sale.
